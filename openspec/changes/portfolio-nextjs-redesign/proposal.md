## Why

The portfolio needs a modern, high-performance presentation layer that communicates product-quality engineering work to recruiters and hiring managers while remaining easy to maintain. A polished Next.js experience will improve first impressions, increase engagement, and provide a scalable foundation for future case studies and updates.

## What Changes

- Build a new production-grade developer portfolio using Next.js App Router, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion.
- Deliver a dark-mode-first experience with a refined visual system, responsive layout, strong typography, and subtle motion.
- Implement a conversion-focused information architecture with hero, projects, skills, experience, and contact sections.
- Introduce a content-driven data model for projects and experience so the site can be updated without rewriting components.
- Provide deployment guidance, Lighthouse optimization notes, and accessibility recommendations for production launch.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `developer-portfolio`: A polished, accessible portfolio experience for showcasing engineering work, skills, experience, and contact paths.

### Modified Capabilities
- None.

## Impact

- New application structure under the frontend app shell, reusable components, and content modules.
- New dependencies for animation, styling, and utility composition.
- Documentation and deployment guidance for Vercel or Netlify-based launch.
